Kape' Bar-Rio — Cashier Turn Over Shift Update

MONDAY–FRIDAY
1st cashier shift -> Turn Over Shift -> 2nd cashier shift -> Close Shop

SATURDAY–SUNDAY
One cashier shift only -> Close Shop. Turn Over Shift is blocked.

RULES
- The opener becomes Cashier on Duty.
- Only Cashier on Duty can save Cash In, Cash Out and Close Shop.
- Turn Over Shift counts actual cash and records Expected / Counted / Difference for the outgoing cashier.
- Incoming cashier is selected and both outgoing + incoming type their Drink PINs.
- Every other person CURRENTLY CLOCKED IN must type their own Drink PIN to acknowledge the count.
- Turnover cannot complete until every required acknowledgement is complete.
- The counted amount becomes the incoming cashier's opening drawer.
- The shop remains OPEN during turnover.

INSTALL
1. Run 01-cashier-turnover-shifts.sql ONCE in Supabase SQL Editor.
2. Replace/add in Home/Open-End-Shift: shifts.js, turnover-shift.js, sync.css, open-end-shift.html, sw.js.
3. Hard refresh once; for installed PWA, fully close and reopen.

Requires your existing cashier verification and attendance system.
