Terms & Conditions

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Online orders

Orders submitted through Kape’ Bar-Rio’s online ordering page are requests to prepare food and drinks for pickup or delivery. Orders remain subject to product availability, shop capacity, delivery coverage, and operating hours.

2. Order information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate contact, pickup, delivery, and payment information. Incorrect or incomplete details may delay or prevent fulfillment.

3. Payments

Available payment methods may include Cash, GCash, and KBR Points where eligible. Orders above the app’s configured cash limit may require GCash. A reward item paid fully by points will appear as ₱0 in the order because its value was paid through KBR Points.

4. KBR Points

KBR Points are a promotional loyalty benefit, not cash and not a bank balance. Points may be earned only on eligible purchases and may be redeemed only under the current rewards rules shown in the app. Food, ice cream, delivery fees, or other excluded items may not earn or accept points where indicated.

5. Cancellations and refunds

If an online order is cancelled before completion, eligible redeemed KBR Points should be returned to the customer account. Cash or GCash refunds, when applicable, are handled according to the actual payment status of the order.

6. Prices and availability

Menu prices, sizes, rewards, delivery areas, fees, and product availability may change. The amount confirmed by the system at the time the order is accepted is the applicable amount for that order.

7. Account use

Customers are responsible for keeping their password and account access secure. Kape’ Bar-Rio may restrict or correct accounts used for abuse, fraud, duplicate rewards, or unauthorized activity.

8. Contact

Questions about an order or account may be sent to kapebarrio.sq@gmail.com or through Kape’ Bar-Rio’s official Messenger account.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Kape’ Bar-Rio respects customer privacy and processes personal data in line with the Philippines’ Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), including the principles of transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect your name, mobile number, email address, Google account identity, saved barangay and delivery address, order history, payment method, loyalty points, account activity, and information needed to fulfill an order.

2. Why we use it

We use customer data to create and secure accounts, receive and prepare orders, contact customers about pickup or delivery, maintain order history, calculate and redeem KBR Points, prevent abuse, troubleshoot the service, and comply with legal or business recordkeeping requirements.

3. Service providers

The ordering system uses service providers such as Supabase for authentication, database storage, and application services, and Google when a customer chooses Google sign-in. These providers may process information needed to provide those services.

4. Sharing

We do not sell customer personal data. Information may be shared only when needed to operate the ordering service, fulfill a delivery, comply with law, protect customers or the business, or work with service providers acting for Kape’ Bar-Rio.

5. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for account, order, loyalty, business, security, and legal purposes. Data that is no longer needed should be deleted, anonymized, or otherwise securely handled when appropriate.

6. Your rights

Under Philippine data privacy law, customers may have rights including being informed, accessing their data, correcting inaccurate data, objecting to certain processing, requesting erasure or blocking where applicable, data portability, filing a complaint, and seeking damages where provided by law.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect customer information. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are not displayed to Kape’ Bar-Rio staff.

8. Privacy requests

For privacy questions or requests concerning your Kape’ Bar-Rio customer account, contact kapebarrio.sq@gmail.com.
